# Suggested updates to the fertility patient-education materials

Prepared for Estella by Helen Wu, October 30, 2026. While building the fertility preservation decision tool, we read every document closely and noticed a few places where the materials have fallen behind the law and the resource landscape. Sharing in case a refresh is on the roadmap.

## "Ability to Have Children: Fertility Issues" info sheet (ref 20000-N-51433)

1. **The financial section predates California's coverage mandate.** The sheet says sperm banking, egg freezing and embryo freezing are "usually not covered" by insurance. Since SB 600 (2019), California fully-insured commercial plans are required to cover medically indicated fertility preservation for patients at risk of treatment-caused infertility (DMHC, under the Knox-Keene Act). Patients reading the current sheet may assume they have to pay cash when they may be covered. A good current reference is the UC San Diego Moores Cancer Center and Alliance for Fertility Preservation toolkit, "Fertility Preservation Benefit Mandates" (updated July 31, 2025), which includes a patient phone script for verifying benefits and a California appeal template.
2. **Fertile Hope no longer exists as its own organization.** The sheet directs patients to Fertile Hope (fertilehope.org) and its Sharing Hope program. Fertile Hope was absorbed into Livestrong; the current program is Livestrong Fertility. The UCSD/AFP toolkit also has an up-to-date financial assistance table with eligibility details (Chick Mission, Team Maggie's Dream, Worth the Wait, Verna's Purse, ReUnite, and others).
3. **Small copy error.** The Radiation section opens with a sentence carried over from the Chemotherapy section: "For patients receiving chemotherapy, age is an important." It looks like it was meant to introduce radiation-specific factors.
4. **The references are cancer.gov pages from June 2017.** Worth refreshing against the current ASCO fertility preservation guideline (2025 update).

## "Fertility Resources" flyer (ref 20000-NEW-38076)

5. **URL typo.** California Fertility Partners is printed as "califroniafertilitypartners.com."
6. **Contact info is undated.** The flyer has no publication or review date, so there is no way to tell how current the phone numbers are. Adding a "last reviewed" date would help patients and staff.

None of this diminishes the materials; the clinical content held up well. These are the spots where 2019-2026 changes in law and organizations have moved out from under the text.
