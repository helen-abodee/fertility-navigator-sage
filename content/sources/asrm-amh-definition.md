# ASRM sources pulled 2026-11-22 (AMH framing, egg-count model, definition)

1. **Ovarian reserve**: ASRM Practice Committee, "Testing and interpreting
   measures of ovarian reserve: a committee opinion," Fertil Steril
   2020;114(6):1151-7, PMID 33280722. Abstract read via PubMed. Key verbatim:
   markers "can be useful as predictors of oocyte yield following controlled
   ovarian stimulation and oocyte retrieval. However, they are poor
   predictors of reproductive potential independently from age."
2. **Egg-count model**: Goldman RH et al., "Predicting the likelihood of live
   birth for elective oocyte cryopreservation: a counseling tool," Hum Reprod
   2017;32(4):853-9, PMID 28166330. Abstract read: evidence-based model of
   live-birth probability from age at retrieval and number of mature oocytes
   frozen. The "about 15 to 20 eggs per hoped-for child under 35" planning
   habit is cited to the REI consultation, not to this paper.
3. **Definition of infertility (2023)**: ASRM Practice Committee PDF read in
   full (asrm.org, 2023; replaces 2020 version, PMID 32115183). Verbatim:
   infertility includes "the need for medical intervention, including, but
   not limited to, the use of donor gametes or donor embryos in order to
   achieve a successful pregnancy either as an individual or with a partner,"
   and "Nothing in this definition shall be used to deny or delay treatment
   to any individual, regardless of relationship status or sexual
   orientation."
4. **Consult citations**: reiConsult() = Stanford reproductive endocrinology
   consultation (2026-11); psychConsult() = Stanford fertility psychology
   consultation (2026-11). Used for practice-pattern facts (urgent oncology
   slots, 15-20 egg planning, $30k real-world IVF cycles, reusing outside
   results, PCP-started workup, therapy access routes) that have no published
   patient-grade source. Same pattern as the social-work consult citation.
