# UCSD patient-facing oncofertility resources — pick-list for "Read more" links

Survey of UC San Diego Health, Moores Cancer Center, oncofertilityrisk.com (CRISP), and Su lab properties. All pages opened and verified 2026-11-03. This is a candidate list for the product owner to choose from, not an integration.

## (a) Great for "Read more" links

1. **Fertility Preservation & Counseling — UC San Diego Health**
   - URL: https://health.ucsd.edu/care/cancer/fertility-preservation-counseling/
   - What a patient gets: the core UCSD oncofertility service page. Explains reproductive risk counseling and every preservation option by sex (embryo/oocyte banking, fertility-sparing surgery, ovarian suppression/GnRH agonists, ovarian tissue banking; sperm and testicular tissue banking), with direct phone numbers (female 858-657-8561, male 858-249-3537) and a link to the insurance guide.
   - Patient-level: yes, fully. No content date shown. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

2. **Fertility Preservation (risk predictor + options tool) — CRISP (oncofertilityrisk.com)**
   - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/infertility/
   - What a patient gets: the Cancer-Related Infertility Score Predictor (infertility risk by age, cancer type, treatment, drugs) plus a filterable "Fertility Preservation Options" tool by cancer type and treatment status; results printable/saveable as PDF; links to SART.org to find a clinic. Clear disclaimers that it does not replace clinical care.
   - Patient-level: yes (written for survivors; plain language). No last-updated date shown. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

3. **Pregnancy After Cancer — CRISP**
   - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/pregnancy/
   - What a patient gets: how to assess fertility after treatment (cycle tracking, fertility testing), pregnancy safety after cancer, baby health risks, breastfeeding compatibility; glossary of medical terms.
   - Patient-level: yes. No date shown. Minor issues: a couple of truncated sentences in the fertility section, and the cancer-type filter shows no results until submitted (may look empty). Retrieved 2026-11-03.

4. **Birth Control — CRISP**
   - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/birth-control/
   - What a patient gets: contraception recommendations filtered by cancer type and treatment status, a clear Discuss / Decide / Start workflow, and a bedsider.org handoff for method details.
   - Patient-level: yes. No last-updated date shown. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

5. **Using Insurance for Fertility Preservation: A Patient Guide (PDF, 23 pp.)**
   - URL: https://health.ucsd.edu/globalassets/content/primary-specialty-care/fertility-care/fertilitypreservationtoolkit.pdf
   - What a patient gets: a step-by-step benefit-verification script (insurance type, coverage, lifetime maximums, in-network clinics, out-of-network coverage, out-of-pocket costs, pharmacy benefits, appeals), with companion YouTube video chapters. Prepared by UCSD and the Alliance for Fertility Preservation; citation dated 2025 (Yoeun, McMenamin, … Su).
   - Patient-level: yes, explicitly "A Patient Guide." Retrieved 2026-11-03.

6. **Male Fertility Tests & Treatment — UC San Diego Health**
   - URL: https://health.ucsd.edu/care/fertility/male-fertility/
   - What a patient gets: therapeutic sperm banking before cancer treatment, cryopreservation of sperm and reproductive tissue, the andrology lab, contact 858-657-7876. The best male-specific preservation page UCSD has.
   - Patient-level: yes. No content date (site copyright 2026). Retrieved 2026-11-03.

7. **CRISP home (English) — oncofertilityrisk.com**
   - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/
   - What a patient gets: entry point to all CRISP tools (fertility preservation, birth control, pregnancy after cancer, menopause, CED calculator, toolkits); Spanish version at /es/home-espanol/. Run out of Moores Cancer Center (Dr. Irene Su's group), funded by the California Breast Cancer Research Program.
   - Patient-level: yes. Useful as the single umbrella link if only one CRISP link is wanted. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

## (b) Usable but narrower

8. **Patient Toolkit — Fertility Preservation Benefit Mandates — CRISP Toolkits page**
   - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/toolkits/
   - What a patient gets: a 2025 downloadable Patient Toolkit on navigating fertility-preservation insurance mandates (plus an Advocate Toolkit). The other six toolkits on the page are for legislators, regulators, insurers, clinics, researchers — not patients.
   - Narrow because: insurance-mandate focus (largely California), and the page mixes patient and policy audiences. Published 2025. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

9. **CED Calculator — CRISP**
   - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/ced-calculator/
   - What a patient gets: computes cyclophosphamide equivalent dose from chemo doses (Green et al. 2013 methodology), which feeds fertility-risk assessment.
   - Narrow because: requires knowing exact chemo doses in mg/m2, gives no interpretation of the resulting number, and is as much a provider tool as a patient one. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

10. **Female Fertility Tests & Treatments — UC San Diego Health**
    - URL: https://health.ucsd.edu/care/fertility/female-fertility/
    - What a patient gets: general fertility workup and treatment (IUI, ovulation induction, surgery); egg/embryo freezing is delivered via partner Reproductive Partners Fertility Center, mentioned but not detailed. Contact 858-657-8745.
    - Narrow because: general infertility page, not oncofertility-specific. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

11. **Fertility Care (hub) — UC San Diego Health**
    - URL: https://health.ucsd.edu/care/fertility/
    - What a patient gets: hub linking female fertility, male fertility, and fertility preservation for cancer patients, with scheduling and insurance guidance.
    - Narrow because: thin hub; the subpages above are the real content. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

12. **Patient and Family Resource Center — Moores Cancer Center**
    - URL: https://health.ucsd.edu/care/cancer/support-services/resource-center/
    - What a patient gets: in-person resource center (English/Spanish materials, trained survivor volunteers, 858-822-6152); links to Fertility Preservation & Counseling.
    - Narrow because: about an on-site service with limited hours, not online education. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

13. **About Us — CRISP**
    - URL: https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/about/
    - What a patient gets: credibility context — team of 14+ UCSD Moores clinicians/researchers led by Dr. Irene Su, CBCRP-funded. Useful as a trust signal next to CRISP links, not as education itself. Retrieved 2026-11-03.

## (c) Skip (and why)

14. **Menopause — CRISP** — https://www.oncofertilityrisk.com/en/menopause/
    - Two PDFs (Hot Flashes, Sexual Health; English + Spanish) explicitly "current through December 2019" — nearly 7 years stale — and some PDF links appear to have empty hrefs. Skip unless refreshed.

15. **Su Lab research page** — https://obgyn.ucsd.edu/research/labs/su/research.html
    - Research/provider-facing lab page (studies, mandates, "Let's TOC Fertility" telehealth trial). Interesting background, wrong register for a patient "Read more" link.

16. **youngcancersurvivor.com (Reproductive Window study patient site)** — DNS does not resolve as of 2026-11-03 (with and without www). The study's public patient site is defunct; older UCSD/Shady Grove materials still cite it. Do not link; study contact was ayastudy@ucsd.edu / 858-822-0768 if ever needed.

17. **clinicaltrials.ucsd.edu/trial/NCT01843140 (FIRST registry)** — registry listing written in trial-protocol language; recruitment-oriented, not education. Skip for "Read more."

## Cross-cutting notes

- No CRISP page shows a last-updated date (the menopause page is the exception, and it is old). If freshness matters in-app, label CRISP links with "retrieved 2026-11-03" internally and re-verify periodically.
- CRISP has full Spanish coverage via /es/home-espanol/ — relevant if the app localizes.
- UCSD Health pages carry no content dates either (only 2026 site copyright), but are maintained service pages and low-risk.
- The two UCSD phone lines for fertility preservation (female 858-657-8561, male 858-249-3537) appear consistently and could be surfaced as a "talk to someone" CTA rather than a link.
