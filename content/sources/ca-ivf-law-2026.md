# California SB 729 (2024) — the "2026 California IVF law"

Research notes for the fertility preservation decision tool. All facts cited; retrieved 2026-10-30 unless noted. Where a point could not be verified against a primary source, that is stated explicitly.

**Access note:** leginfo.legislature.ca.gov and dmhc.ca.gov blocked automated retrieval (Cloudflare 403) on 2026-10-30. Statutory text was verified through CalMatters Digital Democracy (which mirrors the chaptered leginfo text) and a Sharp Health Plan mirror of DMHC materials. ## 1. Bill identity and effective dates

- **Bill:** SB 729 (Menjivar), "Health care coverage: treatment for infertility and fertility services." 2023–2024 session.
  - Chaptered text (primary, human-verify): https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240SB729 (blocked to automation 2026-10-30)
  - Mirror used for text verification: https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202320240sb729 (retrieved 2026-10-30)
- **Signed:** September 29, 2024, by Governor Newsom; chaptered same day as **Chapter 930, Statutes of 2024**.
  - Governor's press release: https://www.gov.ca.gov/2024/09/29/as-republicans-continue-to-block-the-right-to-ivf-governor-newsom-signs-legislation-to-expand-access (located via search 2026-10-30)
  - Chapter number: CalMatters Digital Democracy bill page (retrieved 2026-10-30)
- **Original effective date:** July 1, 2025 (as chaptered).
- **Effective date WAS delayed:** AB 116 (budget trailer bill), signed **June 30, 2025**, moved the effective date from July 1, 2025 to **January 1, 2026**.
  - Primary: Sen. Menjivar's office, "California State Budget Delays Implementation of SB 729," https://sd20.senate.ca.gov/news/california-state-budget-delays-implementation-sb-729-infertility-treatment-health-care (retrieved 2026-10-30) — confirms AB 116, 6-month delay, new date Jan 1, 2026.
  - Corroborated by DMHC-adjacent provider notice: Sharp Health Plan provider alert re APL 25-021, https://www.sharphealthplan.com/docs/default-source/providers/updates-news/2026/all-plan-letter---apl-25-021---implementation-of-senate-bill-729-(2024).pdf (retrieved 2026-10-30): "SB 729 originally carried an effective date of July 1, 2025, which was later delayed to January 1, 2026, pursuant to Assembly Bill 116."
- **Renewal-based application:** the mandate applies to plan contracts **issued, amended, or renewed on or after January 1, 2026** — so an enrollee whose plan renews mid-year does not get the benefit until renewal. Source: RESOLVE SB 729 FAQ, https://resolve.org/learn/financial-resources/insurance-coverage/understanding-californias-ivf-insurance-law/ (retrieved 2026-10-30): "Your coverage started, or will start, when your plan renews on or after January 1, 2026."
- **CalPERS:** delayed until **July 1, 2027** (in the statute itself: "shall not apply ... until July 1, 2027"). Source: CalMatters chaptered-text summary (retrieved 2026-10-30).

## 2. Who is covered and what is required

Amends **Health & Safety Code § 1374.55** (DMHC-regulated health care service plans) **and Insurance Code § 10119.6** (CDI-regulated disability/health insurance policies). Source: CalMatters chaptered text (retrieved 2026-10-30).

| Plan type | Covered? |
|---|---|
| Large group, fully insured (DMHC or CDI) | **Yes — must provide** coverage for diagnosis and treatment of infertility and fertility services, incl. IVF |
| Small group, fully insured | **Offer only** — statute: "shall offer coverage ... This paragraph shall not be construed to require a small group ... to provide coverage." Employer decides whether to buy it. |
| Individual market | **No** — individual-plan requirements were removed from the chaptered version |
| Self-funded employer plans (ERISA) | **No** — state mandate does not reach them (RESOLVE FAQ, retrieved 2026-10-30) |
| Medi-Cal | **No** — statute: "This section does not apply to Medi-Cal managed care health care service plan contracts." |
| Religious employers | Exempt (statutory religious-employer exemption) |

Sources: CalMatters chaptered text (retrieved 2026-10-30); RESOLVE FAQ (retrieved 2026-10-30).

**What must be covered (large group):**
- Diagnosis and treatment of infertility and fertility services, with IVF included — **maximum of three completed oocyte retrievals with unlimited embryo transfers**, in accordance with ASRM guidelines, single embryo transfer "when recommended and medically appropriate." (CalMatters chaptered text, retrieved 2026-10-30.)
- DMHC guidance (APL 25-021) names IVF, ICSI, in vitro maturation, embryo biopsy, assisted hatching, preimplantation genetic testing, IUI/ICI as covered services; minimum of three completed egg retrievals and three sperm retrieval attempts; unlimited medically appropriate embryo transfers. (Word & Brown broker update, https://jrreport.wordandbrown.com/2026/10/27/update-on-californias-sb-729-infertility-and-ivf-coverage-requirement-what-brokers-need-to-know/, retrieved 2026-10-30; APL itself on dmhc.ca.gov was blocked to automation.)

**Definition of infertility (HSC § 1374.55(b), as quoted via CalMatters, retrieved 2026-10-30):** a condition or status characterized by any of:
1. "A licensed physician's findings, based on a patient's medical, sexual, and reproductive history, age, physical findings, diagnostic testing, or any combination of those factors."
2. "A person's inability to reproduce either as an individual or with their partner without medical intervention."
3. "The failure to establish a pregnancy or to carry a pregnancy to live birth after regular, unprotected sexual intercourse" (12 months if under 35; 6 months if 35+).

Prong 2 is what brings in single people and same-sex couples without any medical diagnosis.

**Marital status / sexual orientation:** explicit nondiscrimination clause — coverage must be provided "without discrimination on the basis of age, ancestry, color, disability, domestic partner status, gender, gender expression, gender identity, genetic information, marital status, national origin, race, religion, sex, or sexual orientation." (CalMatters chaptered text, retrieved 2026-10-30. Also Governor's press release: definition "inclusive of LGBTQ+ family planning experiences.")

**Fertility preservation under SB 729:** the statute does not separately define "fertility services," and the chaptered text reviewed does not contain a standalone fertility preservation benefit (CalMatters, retrieved 2026-10-30). Secondary explainers state that **medically necessary fertility preservation (e.g., egg/sperm freezing before cancer treatment) is covered** under SB 729 plans, while elective/social egg freezing is not (RESOLVE FAQ, retrieved 2026-10-30; search-result summaries of fertility-clinic explainers, 2026-10-30). The firmer legal hook for preservation remains SB 600 (below). Treat "SB 729 covers preservation" as supported-by-secondary-sources, not verified against statutory text.

## 3. Relationship to SB 600 (2019)

- **SB 600 (Portantino), Chapter 853, Statutes of 2019, signed October 12, 2019.** Clarifies ("declaratory of existing law") that when a covered treatment may directly or indirectly cause **iatrogenic infertility** (infertility caused by surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, or other medical treatment), **standard fertility preservation services are a basic health care service** — and are *not* "treatment of infertility" (so the old infertility exclusions couldn't be used to deny them). Applies to DMHC plans and CDI policies; does **not** apply to Medi-Cal managed care. Source: https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_201920200sb600 (retrieved 2026-10-30); primary text (human-verify): https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=201920200SB600.
- They **overlap but do different jobs**, and both now live in the same code sections (the iatrogenic-preservation language quoted in current plan manuals sits alongside the SB 729 mandate in § 1374.55 — seen quoted in Medi-Cal plan exclusion pages, search 2026-10-30):
  - **(a) Cancer patient about to start gonadotoxic treatment:** SB 600 is the operative guarantee — fertility preservation (egg/sperm/embryo freezing) is a basic health care service *now*, before treatment, in any DMHC/CDI-regulated commercial plan regardless of group size, and it has been since 2019. SB 729 adds (for large-group plans renewing on/after 1/1/2026): clearer coverage of downstream services, and DMHC's APL 25-021 adds rules for cryopreservation **storage** of eggs/sperm/embryos (including out-of-state vendors) — storage was a notorious gap under SB 600. (Sharp Health Plan alert re revised APL, retrieved 2026-10-30.)
  - **(b) Survivor with treatment-caused infertility years later:** SB 600 does *not* help — it covers preservation before/at the time of the gonadotoxic treatment, not later treatment of established infertility. SB 729 is the new benefit: a survivor in a large-group fully insured plan (renewed on/after 1/1/2026) gets diagnosis, treatment, and IVF (3 completed retrievals, unlimited transfers), and qualifies under the definition via physician findings or inability to reproduce without medical intervention — no 6/12-month "trying" clock needed where findings establish it.

## 4. Medi-Cal

**Still No, as of 2026-10-30.** The UCSD/AFP toolkit statement (Medi-Cal = No, as of June 1, 2025) remains correct:
- SB 729 expressly excludes Medi-Cal managed care contracts (statute, via CalMatters, retrieved 2026-10-30).
- SB 600's preservation mandate also expressly excludes Medi-Cal managed care and DHCS contractors (CalMatters SB 600 page, retrieved 2026-10-30).
- Medi-Cal plan documents currently list infertility treatment and fertility preservation among non-covered services (e.g., Health Net Medi-Cal provider manual "Principal Exclusions and Limitations," https://providerlibrary.healthnetcalifornia.com/medi-cal/provider-manual/benefits/principal-exclusions-limitations.html; L.A. Care "Services you cannot get through L.A. Care or Medi-Cal," https://lacare.org/members/handbook/services-you-cannot-get-through-la-care-or-medi-cal — located via search 2026-10-30).
- No DHCS announcement adding IVF or fertility preservation to Medi-Cal was found in searches on 2026-10-30. (Absence of evidence noted as such, not proof; re-check DHCS before publishing.)

## 5. Regulatory guidance (DMHC APL 25-021) — timeline

- **Original APL 25-021** issued by DMHC in **December 2025** (per Word & Brown broker update, retrieved 2026-10-30; not verified against dmhc.ca.gov directly — blocked).
- **Revised 2026-02-23** (revision date in the DMHC filename "REVISED(2_23_2026)" and in Sharp Health Plan's 2026-03-20 provider alert, retrieved 2026-10-30). Clarified definitions, lifetime limits (allowed for large group within rules; small group products may not impose lifetime limits on the specified services), cryopreservation/storage incl. out-of-state vendors, donor/surrogate services, enrollee plan changes.
- **Revised again 2026-10-05**, with a companion FAQ; those revisions take effect for contracts issued/amended/renewed **on or after July 1, 2027**. Changes: how completed egg/sperm retrievals count toward minimums, embryo creation explicitly covered, donor egg/sperm procurement minimums, donor/surrogate infertility services covered by the enrollee's plan, broader protection for people with prior elective sterilization (including gender-affirming care). (Word & Brown, 2026-10-27 article, retrieved 2026-10-30.)
- APL PDF (primary, human-verify; blocked to automation): https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/Portals/0/Docs/OPL/APL25-021-ImplementationofSenateBill729(2024)-REVISED(2_23_2026).pdf

## Open questions

1. **CDI side is thinner.** SB 729 amends Insurance Code § 10119.6, but CDI has not issued guidance equivalent to DMHC's APL 25-021; coverage of some advanced procedures (e.g., PGT) may differ for CDI-regulated policies (Word & Brown, retrieved 2026-10-30; RESOLVE FAQ, retrieved 2026-10-30). Not yet verified on insurance.ca.gov (CDI 2024 press release exists at https://www.insurance.ca.gov/0400-news/0100-press-releases/2024/release045-2024.cfm — not fetched).
2. **Fertility preservation inside SB 729:** secondary sources say medically necessary preservation is covered, but "fertility services" is undefined in the statute; the clean statutory basis for preservation remains SB 600. Verify against the full current text of HSC § 1374.55 / Ins. Code § 10119.6 on leginfo (blocked to automation this session).
3. **Original APL issuance date (December 2025)** rests on a single broker source; confirm on dmhc.ca.gov.
4. **Oct 2026 APL revision** terms don't bind until July 1, 2027 — between now and then, donor/surrogate and retrieval-counting details follow the Feb 2026 APL version.
5. **Mid-year renewals:** many large-group enrollees did not actually gain coverage until their 2026 renewal date; tool copy should ask "when does your plan year renew?"
6. **Medi-Cal:** no coverage change found, but re-check DHCS (dhcs.ca.gov) and the 2026-27 budget before each content release.
7. **Religious employer exemption scope** (exact definition of "religious employer") not pulled from statute text; verify on leginfo.
