# Catherine Benedict: Roadmap to Parenthood (family building AFTER cancer)

Recommended by Dr. Su (2026-11-10); Helen reading the study 2026-11-17.

- What it is: a web-based decision aid and planning tool for family building
  after cancer, for female young adult survivors, from Stanford
  (roadmaptoparenthood.stanford.edu).
- Papers located (full text not yet read by Claude; JMIR blocked automated
  access):
  1. Benedict C, et al. A Decision Aid Intervention for Family Building After
     Cancer. JMIR Form Res 2021;5(1):e20841.
  2. Benedict C, et al. Development of a Web-Based Decision Aid and Planning
     Tool for Family Building After Cancer (Roadmap to Parenthood): Usability
     Testing. JMIR Cancer 2022;8(2):e33304.
  3. Preliminary testing of "Roadmap to Parenthood"... single-arm pilot
     (2024); pilot RCT registered as NCT04059237.
- Reported outcomes (from study pages/abstracts): improvements in decisional
  conflict, knowledge, self-efficacy, and patient-provider communication;
  fertility distress and avoidance relate to poorer family-building decisions.
- TO CONFIRM against the paper Helen is reading: the exact module list
  (expected: fertility after cancer, testing, IVF with own or donor eggs,
  surrogacy, adoption, choosing not to parent, emotional coping, financial
  planning, and a stepwise planning tool).
- Relevance: the model for a finished-treatment "paths to parenthood" section;
  also measurement lineage (Benedict works in the same RCAC family).
