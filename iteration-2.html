How the risk assessment works

It returns a named group from published guidelines, never a percentage for the person: Higher risk group, In-between, Lower, Depends on your dose, or Not enough to say. Sources: the Livestrong/Fertile Hope risk charts (recovered in full from archived PDFs), the ESMO 2020 guideline tables, and the Children's Oncology Group survivorship guidelines. The full extraction, including conflicts between sources, lives in content/sources/risk-framework.md.

The honest part: every published framework keys its fine detail to total dose and cycle count, which patients don't know. So the tool confidently bands what is bandable without numbers (transplant conditioning is high everywhere; an alkylating drug plus pelvic radiation is high; cisplatin plans are intermediate for ovaries; cyclophosphamide breast regimens under 30 are lower) and says "depends on your dose, ask your team for it (they may call it CED)" for the rest, instead of guessing. Every answer ends with the same line: this is a guideline group, not a prediction about you.
