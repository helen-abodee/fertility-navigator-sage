What's inside

  • All 10 questions with back, progress, skip, and "not sure" everywhere; survivor fork and partner-only insurance question. The anti-anxiety rule is enforced in code: a skipped or "not sure" answer can never hide content, only a definite answer narrows it.
  • Cited content: 7 preservation options (steps, timelines, honest expectations, costs), 8 guidance sections (SB 600 script, SB 729 for survivors, Medi-Cal honesty, benefit verification, survivor labs by anatomy, semen collection prep, partner plans), 11 aid programs, 16 audience-gated doctor questions. Sources: UCSD/AFP toolkit (July 31, 2025), ASCO 2025 guideline, SB 729 statute research, MSKCC, ASRM, COG, ACS.
  • Clinic directory: all 16 flyer clinics with the flyer's own words (never an endorsement), plus UC San Diego; 12 HRC Southern California offices under one record; 5 clinics with real published prices, the rest honestly "not published."
  • Privacy held: no backend, answers in memory, opt-in save to local storage with erase, ZIP distance via a bundled public-domain Census table, map tiles from OpenStreetMap with no key.
  • Risk module: returns "coming soon" for every input by test, pending Hannah's document.

Found while building (for your radar)

  • The flyer's clinic world has shifted: California Fertility Partners is now Pinnacle Fertility Los Angeles; USC Fertility was absorbed into HRC in 2021; the Oncofertility Consortium moved from Northwestern to Michigan State; California Cryobank's oncofertility site (fertile-future.com) is gone. All recorded with proving URLs in content/sources/clinics-provenance.md. Worth adding to the Estella note later.
  • Two sites blocked fetching (USC Fertility returns errors, Western Fertility blocks bots), so their fields read "not published" until checked by hand.
  • Content gaps the writers hit (TESE patient-level detail, GnRH agonist cost, most aid caps) are appended to content/GAPS.md items 16 to 22.
