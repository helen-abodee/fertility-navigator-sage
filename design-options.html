C · Lagoon, one accent

The simplest system: a single blue-green teal that reads as both colors at once, on a quiet stone paper. One accent means the fewest decisions and the most even, "objective" feel across screens; it harmonizes with City of Hope blue without ever competing with it. The trade: less expressive range for states like success vs. info.
