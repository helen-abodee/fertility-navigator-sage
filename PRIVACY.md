# How this tool protects your privacy

Last reviewed: October 30, 2026.

**Your answers stay on your device.** When you answer questions here, the answers live only in your browser's memory. When you close the tab, they are gone. There is no account to create, no database, and no server that receives your answers.

**"Save my progress" is optional and local.** If you choose to save your progress, your answers are stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. The "Erase everything" button removes all of it immediately.

**Your summary PDF is made on your device.** When you download your summary, your browser builds the PDF itself. The information in it is never uploaded.

**Your ZIP code never leaves your phone.** If you enter a ZIP code, it is used only to measure the distance to clinics on the map, and only on your device.

**No trackers.** This site has no advertising trackers, no social media pixels, and no third-party analytics SDKs. If we ever add usage analytics, they will count page views only and will never receive any of your answers.

**What we can see:** nothing about you. We cannot see your answers, your age, your diagnosis, or your results, because they are never sent to us.

**A note on medical advice:** this tool is for information. It does not diagnose anything, predict your fertility, or tell you what to do. Decisions about fertility preservation belong with you and your care team.
