The entry groups

The first screen is now "Where are you right now?" with five lanes, each calibrating depth:

  • Diagnosed, no treatment plan yet: a short flow with zero treatment-detail questions, an honest "we can't place your risk yet," and the questions to bring to the planning appointment.
  • I have a treatment plan / in treatment: the full flow, including the CRISP regimen question.
  • I finished cancer treatment: the survivor spine.
  • I'm here for someone I care about: caregivers walk the flow on the person's behalf.
  • Just learning for now: straight to the browse hub, no personal questions at all.

Also fixed per your question: the first screen and every ambiguous spot say cancer treatment. And on the lymphoma name: your memory is right that it used to be "non-Hodgkin's"; NCI's current style drops the apostrophe, which is what we use (the UCSD tool still uses the older form).

The design scheme: three sections, three moods

1 · You and your treatment

The existing fir-on-paper: clinical, calm, respectful. Icons joined the answers (egg, syringe, radiation, scalpel).

2 · Children, someday

Warm clay and soft amber tints on a warmer paper. Quietly hopeful, never pushy; the milestone into it says "there is no wrong answer."

3 · The practical side

Slate blue: matter-of-fact money-and-logistics mood. Only shown at all if children are wanted or possible; a "no" ends the survey with dignity.

Between sections, a milestone screen takes a breath, names what's done, and carries the next section's palette. Progress dots and all controls re-theme per section automatically (dark mode included).

The thinking-it-through path

Answering "I'm not sure" to the children question now offers an optional 8-screen reflection, every prompt traceable to a published decision aid (content/sources/parenthood-decision-aids.md): the Ottawa Personal Decision Guide as the backbone (free with attribution), CDC's reproductive life plan (public domain), and the BEFORE and Cancer-Fertility-and-Me oncofertility aids. The spine: it's two decisions with two clocks; locate yourself (torn is a legal square); what parenthood could mean; plans are allowed to change; what preserving does and doesn't promise; the other roads to parenthood; a what-matters slider exercise (nothing saved, nothing scored); who to talk with, the Ottawa pressure check, and deciding-later-on-purpose. Skippable at the door, and "unsure" stays a finished answer.

The hub

Results are no longer one long scroll: five tabs ("Start here" with your risk line, journey, and doctor questions; Options; Paying for it; Clinics; Health), take them in any order, nothing expires. The journey card also gained "Message my care team": the patient saves their social worker's number or email once (on device), and every stuck moment becomes a one-tap text or email from their own phone, prefilled with which step they're on. That ships now with zero backend; the passive stage-sync version stays in the data-architecture doc pending the BAA conversation.
