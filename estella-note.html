Suggested updates to the fertility patient-education materials

From Helen Wu · October 30, 2026

While building the fertility preservation decision tool, we read every document closely. The clinical content held up well. A few spots, though, have had the law and the resource landscape move out from under them since publication, and we wanted to flag them in case a refresh is on the roadmap.

"Ability to Have Children: Fertility Issues" info sheet (20000-N-51433)

  1. The financial section predates California's coverage mandate. The sheet says sperm banking, egg freezing and embryo freezing are "usually not covered" by insurance. Since SB 600 (2019), California fully-insured commercial plans must cover medically indicated fertility preservation for patients at risk of treatment-caused infertility (DMHC, under the Knox-Keene Act). And starting with plans renewing on or after January 1, 2026, SB 729 adds IVF coverage in large-group plans, which matters for survivors. Patients reading the current sheet may assume they must pay cash when they may be covered.
    A strong current reference: UC San Diego Moores Cancer Center and Alliance for Fertility Preservation, "Fertility Preservation Benefit Mandates" toolkit (updated July 31, 2025). It includes a patient phone script for verifying benefits and a California appeal template.
  2. Fertile Hope no longer exists as its own organization. The sheet directs patients to Fertile Hope and its Sharing Hope program; Fertile Hope was absorbed into Livestrong, and the current program is Livestrong Fertility. The UCSD/AFP toolkit has an up-to-date financial assistance table with eligibility details (Chick Mission, Team Maggie's Dream, Worth the Wait, Verna's Purse, ReUnite, and others).
  3. Small copy error. The Radiation section opens with a sentence carried over from the Chemotherapy section: "For patients receiving chemotherapy, age is an important."
  4. References are cancer.gov pages from June 2017. Worth refreshing against the 2025 update of the ASCO fertility preservation guideline.

"Fertility Resources" flyer (20000-NEW-38076)

  1. URL typo. California Fertility Partners is printed as "califroniafertilitypartners.com."
  2. No review date. The flyer is undated, so there is no way to tell how current the phone numbers are. A "last reviewed" line would help patients and staff.

Happy to share the underlying citations for any of this, or a draft of updated language, whenever useful.
